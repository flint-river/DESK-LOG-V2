What this is

Desk Log is a page you keep open all shift. It does three things: it alarms you for the calls and tasks you must not miss, it keeps your shift notes with time stamps so you can paste them into your SOCR, and it holds screen captures from the shift. Everything you add is saved to a file on your I: drive, so it follows you to whatever desk you sit at tomorrow.

It only alarms while this page is open in a browser tab. Do not close it during your shift.

Every shift: the 4 steps

The blue START HERE box on the Alarms tab walks you through it. In short:

  1. Turn on sound. Browsers will not make noise until you click something on the page. The button beeps so you know it worked. If you do not want noise, click Skip; the screens still flash.
  2. Open your reminders file. Click Open My File and pick desk-log-data.js on your I: drive. First time ever, click I do not have a file yet and save a new one to I:. Skip means you run on the standard list for today only and nothing is saved.
  3. Put an alert window on every screen. Type how many screens you want covered and click Open Alert Windows. Small windows pop up; drag one onto each screen and leave it there. They shrink to a tab in the corner when nothing is going on.
  4. Test it. Click Test Alarm. Every screen should flash and beep. Click ACKNOWLEDGE on any screen to stop it.

Each step turns green with DONE as you do it. The box folds away when all four are done; click the START HERE line to see it again.

Your I: file

Your reminders, your settings, your notes and your captures all live in one file: desk-log-data.js. Keep it on your I: drive. Once it is open (the status line under My Reminders File turns green), every change saves to it by itself about a second later. You never need to click Save, though there is a Save Now button if you want to be sure.

Edge asks for one approval per session to write to that file; that is the Open My File click. There is no way around that, it is how the browser protects your files.

If the file is not open, the page still works, but anything you add today lives only in this desk's browser and is gone when the VDI resets. The status line stays amber to remind you.

Tip: in the file window, your I: drive is in the list on the left, named with your employee number, like 840422$ (\\natladmhqrfs04) (I:). You can also just drag desk-log-data.js from Explorer and drop it anywhere on this page.

Alert windows

The main page can only take over the screen it is on. To cover the others, it opens small helper windows, one per screen. Drag each onto a different monitor. When an alarm fires, each one grows to fill its monitor, flashes and beeps. When you acknowledge, they shrink to a small strip in the bottom-right corner showing the Zulu time and a Notes button.

The strip at the top of each helper window with the web address is put there by Edge and cannot be removed.

Alarm button on a helper window opens a small Quick Add right there: type what it is, build the time with +1 / +5 / +15 / :15 / :30 / :45 / Next Hour, click ADD. It goes straight into the list on the main page as a one-time alarm.

Notes button on a helper window opens a quick note box right there. Type, press Enter, and the note goes into your shift log on the main page with the time stamp. Handy when the main page is buried under something else.

If a helper window gets closed, click Open Alert Windows again. The blue line under that button shows how many are connected. If Edge blocks the popups, click the blocked-popup icon at the right end of the address bar, choose Always allow, and click again.

Keep pulling alert windows to the front (checkbox): while an alarm is live, the windows push themselves back in front every second so an email or Teams window cannot bury them. Uncheck it if you find that annoying.

When an alarm goes off

Every screen flashes with the reminder title in big letters, the time it is for, and your notes. Buttons:

  • ACKNOWLEDGE - handled. A daily reminder rolls to tomorrow. A one-time reminder is removed. If log acked alarms is on (Notes tab), a line goes into your shift log.
  • Snooze 5 / 10 / 15 - comes back in that many minutes, same buttons again.
  • If the reminder has an early alarm (say 2 minutes before), the alarm fires at the early time and there is an extra Snooze 2 (= on time) that lands you exactly on the call time. There is no second alarm at the call time; acknowledge means done, snooze if you want to be told again.

Acknowledging or snoozing on any screen clears all of them.

Quick Add

The bar above the reminder list is for ad hoc things that crop up: type what it is, build the time with the buttons, click ADD (or press Enter). The time starts at right now. +1, +5, +15 add minutes and stack (click +5 three times for 15). :15, :30, :45 and Next Hour jump to the next such mark and also stack. Undo takes back the last press; Reset goes back to now.

Quick Add makes a one-time, on-time, local-time reminder. Those show in the list with a blue tint and a blue bar on the left so they stand out from the standing calls. If you need more (repeat, early alarm, flight details), click Edit on its row.

The edit form

The left column is the full form. It is hidden by default; click the CLICK TO OPEN THE EDIT FORM rail to open it, and the Hide button to put it away. Edit or Duplicate on any row opens it for you.

Everything on it is optional. Fill in what you have:

  • Category - Flight, Conference call, Operational issue, Weather/ATC, Other. Picking Flight opens the flight details box: flight category (diversion, delay, MX and so on), flight number, tail, origin, destination.
  • Title - what shows in big letters. Leave it blank and one is built from the category and flight details, for example Diversion DL1234 ATL-LGA.
  • Notes - bridge number, who to call, what to check. Shown on the alert screen.
  • Fire at - see Time, date, Local vs Zulu below.
  • Early alarm - minutes before the alarm time that you want to be told. 0 means on time.
  • Repeat and Seasonal - see Repeat and seasons below.

Add Reminder saves it. When you got there by Edit, the button reads Save Changes and there is a Cancel Edit next to it.

Time, date, Local vs Zulu

The header shows Local and Zulu clocks. The NOW strip in the form shows them again with seconds, plus how long until the fire time you are building.

DATE defaults to today. Pick a later date for a one-time reminder in the future; the label beside it says tomorrow, 12 days out, and so on.

LOCAL and ZULU boxes both show the fire time. Type in either one (1950 or 19:50 both work) and the other updates. If the date is today and that time has already passed, it rolls to tomorrow.

Buttons: -1 hr through -1 and +1 through +1 hr nudge the time; NOW+5 resets; next :15 / :30 / :45 / top of hour jump; the 24 hour buttons set HH:00 local, then nudge the minutes.

Which clock controls matters twice a year when the clocks change. LOCAL means the reminder keeps its local wall time (09:00L stays 09:00L and the Zulu time shifts an hour). ZULU means it keeps its Zulu time (1400Z stays 1400Z and the local time shifts). It sets itself to whichever box you typed in; you can also click LOCAL or ZULU. The list shows the controlling time in bold color.

Repeat and seasons

Repeat runs from one time through every 15 minutes, hourly, every 2-12 hours, daily, weekdays, weekends, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, every 2 months, quarterly, every 6 months, yearly. Monthly types keep the date and clamp at month end (Jan 31 becomes Feb 28).

Seasonal limits a repeating reminder to part of the year. Either click the month buttons (green = active; All year, Oct - Apr, May - Sep shortcuts) or set exact From and To dates (every year, the year part is ignored, and it can wrap over New Year like Nov 15 to Mar 31). Exact dates win over the month buttons. Out of season the reminder sits dimmed in the list and says when it next fires.

The reminder list

Two buckets: TODAY holds everything still to fire today, in time order, and shrinks as you acknowledge. TOMORROW AND LATER is collapsed; click it to see tomorrow's rollovers, future one-timers and off-season ones.

Columns: Reminder (title, notes, status), Mute, Alarm (local in blue, Zulu in amber, the controlling one in bold, plus the date and which clock controls), In (countdown to when it actually fires), Repeat, Triggers Alarm (2 min before at 13:13L, or On time at 14:30L), Cat (category and flight tag), then the buttons.

Rows turn amber inside 10 minutes and red when due. One-time alarms are tinted blue. Muted and off-season rows are dimmed.

Mute vs ACK vs X

  • Mute - skip this one occurrence. It stays in the list, dimmed, and will not fire. A muted daily comes back unmuted tomorrow. There is a Mute button on both sides of the row.
  • ACK - handled now, without waiting for it to fire. A daily moves to tomorrow (seasonal ones to the next in-season day). A one-time alarm is removed.
  • Edit - opens it in the form. Duplicate - copies it into the form so you can set a new time and add it as a second reminder (same call two hours later, say).
  • X - delete it.

Notes tab

Your running log for the shift. Start typing in the entry box and the date and time stamp themselves in front of what you type (10/28/26 14:38L / 18:38Z - ). Press Enter or click Add to save that note to the log below and start the next one. + Time Stamp drops another stamp inside the box if one note spans some time. The log itself can be edited by clicking into it. Undo and Redo (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y in the log) step back through the last 50 changes, including an accidental delete, a Clear All, or a New Shift archive. Clear All wipes the log after you type YES in the box that pops up (it does not archive; use New Shift for that).

log acked alarms (on by default) adds a stamped line each time you acknowledge an alarm, so the call log writes itself.

Copy for SOCR copies the whole log to the clipboard. New Shift (archive) moves today's notes under Past shifts (the last 14 are kept) and blanks the pad; click a past shift to copy it. If you forget, it happens by itself: notes from a shift that started 14 or more hours ago are archived automatically the next time the page opens, your file is opened, or a new note is added, so a night shift's notes stay together and the next shift starts clean.

The number on the NOTES tab is how many notes you have this shift.

Captures tab

Snip something (Win+Shift+S), click the dashed box, press Ctrl+V. The capture is stamped with the date and time, shrunk to keep your file small, and saved. Type a caption in the box first if you want one, or add it later with the Caption button. Click a thumbnail to see it full size; click anywhere to close. Copy puts it back on the clipboard as a picture for email or Teams. X deletes it.

Up to 30 per shift; the oldest drop off. New Shift (archive) keeps the last 3 shifts, 10 captures each, under Past shifts. Like the notes, captures from 14 or more hours ago are archived automatically.

Sound, color, picture

  • SOUND: ON / OFF in the header. Off means no siren; screens still flash.
  • Alert color (edit form, Multi-Monitor section) - ten color pairs; Preview shows one for 3 seconds without disturbing the alert windows.
  • Backdrop picture - choose an image to show behind the alert text on every screen; the brightness slider keeps the words readable.
  • Number of alert windows and the keep-in-front checkbox are there too.

All of these are saved in your I: file, so your setup follows you.

Defaults, Clear All, Advanced

Load Defaults adds the standard team reminders (the SPO calls, briefings, huddles and so on) to your list, skipping any you already have. A brand-new user starts with them automatically. Clear All deletes every reminder. Advanced opens Export and Import, which copy and paste the whole file as text; use them if the I: file buttons are blocked on a computer, or to send your list to a coworker.

If something is not working

  • No sound - click anywhere on the page once, or the Turn On Sound button. Check the header does not say SOUND: OFF.
  • Only one screen flashes - the alert windows are not open, or were opened from an older copy of the page. Close them and click Open Alert Windows again. The blue count line should match your screens.
  • Nothing pops up - popups are blocked. Click the icon at the right end of the address bar, choose Always allow for this site, click Open Alert Windows again.
  • My reminders are gone - you did not open your I: file this shift. Click Open My File. If you never made one, Load Defaults gets the standard list back.
  • SAVE FAILED in amber - the I: drive dropped. Click Open My File again, or Save Now once it is back.
  • Red bar at the top - you are not in Edge or Chrome. Open the page in one of those.
  • It fired, then fired again at the call time - that was the old behavior. Now it fires once, at the early time if you set one. If you see two, you have an old copy; reload the page.
